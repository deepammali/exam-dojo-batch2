**Objective:** Capture the flag from the traffic on your host, and submit it.

1. Run `/challenge/run`. Your shell moves into the challenge network as host `10.0.0.1`. Type `exit` to leave it.
2. Your host (`10.0.0.1`) is already exchanging traffic on TCP port `31337`, and the flag is in that traffic.
3. Capture the traffic on your host and read the flag out of it.
